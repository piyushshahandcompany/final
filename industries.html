Industries We Serve

Our firm advises clients across diverse industries, delivering industry-relevant accounting, taxation, audit, GST, and compliance solutions backed by deep regulatory understanding.

Industry-Focused Professional Advisory

Every industry operates within a unique regulatory, commercial, and compliance environment. At Piyush Shah and Company, we understand that effective professional advisory requires not only technical knowledge, but also a clear understanding of industry-specific risks, operational realities, and statutory obligations.

Our experience across multiple sectors enables us to deliver practical, compliant, and sustainable solutions tailored to the specific needs of each client segment.

Trading & Distribution Businesses

We advise wholesale and retail trading businesses on accounting, taxation, GST compliance, and working capital management. Our services address issues such as inventory valuation, turnover-based compliance, GST reconciliations, and tax assessments.

We assist traders in maintaining accurate records, meeting statutory deadlines, and managing regulatory exposure in a high-volume, transaction-driven environment.

Manufacturing Companies

Manufacturing entities operate under complex compliance frameworks involving GST, income tax, labour regulations, and statutory audits. We provide audit, accounting, and compliance advisory tailored to manufacturing operations.

Our services focus on cost structures, inventory controls, audit readiness, and regulatory discipline to support operational efficiency and compliance certainty.

Professionals & Practice-Based Businesses

We advise doctors, consultants, architects, lawyers, and other professionals on taxation, accounting, and compliance matters specific to professional practice.

Our support includes income tax planning, return filing, audit requirements, and advisory on practice structuring and regulatory obligations.

Small & Medium Enterprises (SMEs / MSMEs)

SMEs and MSMEs require reliable accounting systems, GST compliance, statutory filings, and advisory support aligned with growth objectives. We provide end-to-end professional services tailored to SME operations.

Our approach focuses on compliance discipline, financial visibility, and structured reporting to support informed business decisions.

Service Sector Businesses

Service providers such as IT firms, logistics companies, consultants, and agencies face sector-specific GST and income tax compliance requirements.

We assist service businesses in managing GST classifications, invoicing, compliance, and audit preparedness with clarity and accuracy.

Startups & New Enterprises

Startups require early-stage guidance on business structuring, registrations, accounting systems, and compliance frameworks. We support startups from inception through operational scaling.

Our advisory ensures statutory compliance, financial discipline, and regulatory readiness during critical growth phases.

Why Industry Experience Matters

Industry experience enables us to anticipate compliance challenges, identify regulatory risks, and deliver advice grounded in practical realities. Our multi-sector exposure allows us to apply best practices while ensuring adherence to applicable laws and professional standards.

Clients benefit from advisory that is not generic, but informed by sector-specific insight and long-standing professional experience.
